# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** October 5, 2026

## 1. Overview

Drink Awareness ("we," "us," "our," or the "Company") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application (the "App").

**Please read this privacy policy carefully.** If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use our App.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide Directly

**Account Information:**
- Email address
- Password (encrypted)
- Age confirmation
- Name (optional)

**Drink Logging Data:**
- Type of beverage (beer, wine, spirits, etc.)
- Alcohol by volume (ABV)
- Quantity consumed
- Time consumed
- Location/venue
- Cost
- Personal notes

**Health & Fitness Data:**
- Steps (from Apple Health/Google Fit)
- Sleep duration (from Apple Health/Google Fit)
- Heart rate (from Apple Health/Google Fit)
- Only when you explicitly connect these services

**Subscription & Payment Data:**
- Subscription plan selected
- Payment method (processed by Stripe, not stored by us)
- Billing address
- Invoice information

### 2.2 Information Collected Automatically

**Usage Information:**
- App features used and frequency
- Time spent in the app
- Screens visited
- Actions performed

**Device Information:**
- Device type (iPhone, Android)
- Operating system version
- Device ID (anonymous)
- App version

**Network Information:**
- IP address (for security purposes)
- Connection type (WiFi, cellular)

### 2.3 Location Information

- We do not collect precise location data
- Venue names are user-provided, not GPS-based
- Location data is never stored or shared

## 3. How We Use Your Information

### 3.1 Core Functionality

- Create and manage your account
- Log and track your drinking data
- Generate personalized AI coaching recommendations
- Sync data across your devices
- Process subscription payments
- Send notifications about milestones

### 3.2 Analytics & Improvement

- Understand how you use the App
- Improve features and functionality
- Fix bugs and optimize performance
- Monitor usage patterns (anonymized)
- Create aggregated statistics

### 3.3 Communications

- Send security alerts
- Notify of payment issues
- Deliver coaching recommendations
- Send milestone celebrations
- Respond to support requests

## 4. Data Storage & Security

### 4.1 Where Data Is Stored

- Your account data: Encrypted in our secure servers
- Drink logging data: Encrypted locally on your device + backed up to our servers
- Health data: Stored locally on your device only
- Payments: Processed by Stripe (never stored by us)

### 4.2 Security Measures

- All data transmitted via HTTPS/TLS encryption
- Passwords hashed with bcrypt
- OAuth tokens stored securely
- Regular security audits
- Compliance with industry standards

### 4.3 Data Retention

- Account data: Stored while account is active
- Drink logs: Stored for historical analytics
- Deleted data: Permanently removed within 30 days
- Backups: Retained for 90 days, then securely destroyed

## 5. Third-Party Services

### 5.1 Services We Use

**Stripe** (Payment Processing)
- Processes subscription payments only
- Does not retain full card information
- GDPR and PCI-DSS compliant
- [Stripe Privacy Policy](https://stripe.com/privacy)

**Anthropic Claude API** (AI Coaching)
- Receives anonymized drink data only
- No personal identifiers sent
- Responses stored on your device
- [Anthropic Privacy Policy](https://www.anthropic.com/privacy)

**Apple Health / Google Fit** (Health Integration)
- Data synced directly from your device
- You control which data is shared
- We never store the full raw health data
- Only aggregated metrics stored

**Expo** (App Distribution)
- Hosts our app builds
- May collect device data for analytics
- [Expo Privacy Policy](https://expo.dev/privacy)

### 5.2 Third-Party Analytics

- We do NOT use Google Analytics
- We do NOT use Facebook Pixel
- We do NOT use any invasive tracking tools
- Limited, privacy-first usage analytics only

## 9. GDPR & International Privacy

### 9.1 GDPR Compliance (EU Users)

You have rights to:
- Access your data (Article 15)
- Rectify incorrect data (Article 16)
- Erasure/right to be forgotten (Article 17)
- Data portability (Article 20)
- Restrict processing (Article 18)
- Object to processing (Article 21)

### 9.2 California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

You have rights to:
- Know what personal data is collected
- Know whether personal data is sold/disclosed
- Delete personal data we collect
- Opt-out of data sales (we don't sell data)
- Non-discrimination for exercising rights

### 9.3 Data Processing Agreement

For enterprise users, we provide Data Processing Agreements covering GDPR Article 28 requirements.
